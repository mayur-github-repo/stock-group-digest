## 1. Frameworks & lessons (reuse these to connect dots)

### 1a. The "hidden asset unlock" pattern (Lloyds Metals & Energy, 2020 → 2026)
LLOYDSME went roughly 180x from its late-2020 price. All six of these conditions held; use
them as a screen:
1. An asset worth far more than the market cap (an iron-ore mining lease).
2. Idle for a **fixable** reason (security problems, litigation, no operator).
3. An operating partner putting in capital (the mine developer became a co-promoter).
4. The unlock lifts margins immediately (captive ore halved raw-material cost).
5. A market much bigger than the company.
6. **Priced for failure:** near-zero profit, stretched balance sheet, low rating.

A capped niche differs from an unbounded market. Most SME IPOs are priced for growth, the
opposite of condition 6. These setups tend to be long-listed microcaps near book value with
a stuck asset and a fresh catalyst. Survivorship bias applies: most hidden assets never
unlock.

### 1b. Reading the group
- Numbers quoted "from a con-call or investor meet" are hearsay until they appear in an
  exchange filing.
- Promoter and insider transactions (bulk deals, SAST/PIT filings) outweigh chat hype.
  Check which way the money actually moved.
